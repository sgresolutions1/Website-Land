Florida land

We buy vacant land in Florida

SGRE Solutions is based in Miami, and we buy vacant land throughout Florida, not just in South Florida. If you own a lot or acreage you no longer want, you can ask us for a cash offer and skip a traditional listing, showings and agent fees.

Florida has a long history of platted lots and inland parcels that were sold to buyers around the country and never built on. On the Gulf Coast, that includes Sarasota County communities such as North Port, Venice and Englewood. In Southwest Florida, Lee County areas like Lehigh Acres and Cape Coral also have many recorded residential lots. Inland, we look at land in Central Florida counties such as Polk, Highlands, Hardee, DeSoto and Okeechobee, where you will find a mix of lots, former agricultural land and rural acreage. Along the Nature Coast and in the northern part of the peninsula, counties such as Citrus, Hernando, Marion, Columbia and Suwannee have wooded tracts and rural parcels around towns like Ocala and Lake City. We also consider vacant land in the Panhandle and in other parts of the state when the property is a fit.

Selling a Florida lot without listing it

Owners often come to us after inheriting a Florida lot, after moving out of state, or after years of paying taxes or association fees on land they do not plan to build on. Some parcels have wetlands, drainage questions, overgrown vegetation or limited road access, which can make a conventional sale slower. We buy land as-is. You do not need to clear the lot, fill anything in, or get a survey just to get an offer.

Requesting an offer is free and comes with no obligation. Tell us the county, the subdivision or parcel if you have it, and the approximate size. We review the land and comparable sales and make a cash offer. We do not charge commissions or seller fees, and we cover closing costs. If the offer works for you, you choose the closing date. If it does not, you can simply say no.

To get started, use the form on this page or call or text (786) 382-3874. Whether the land is in North Port, Lehigh Acres, the Florida Heartland, North Florida or another county, we are glad to take a look.

FAQ

Common questions about selling land in Florida

Do you buy land throughout Florida, or only near Miami?

We are based in Miami, but we buy vacant land throughout Florida, including Gulf Coast and Southwest Florida lots, Central Florida acreage, and rural land in North Florida and the Panhandle when the parcel is a fit. We also buy land nationwide.

Do you buy platted lots in places like North Port or Lehigh Acres?

Yes. We buy vacant platted lots as well as larger acreage. If you own one lot or several, tell us the county, subdivision or parcel ID and we will take a look.

What if the lot has wetlands, drainage or access issues?

We buy land as-is. Wetlands, drainage questions, overgrown lots or limited access do not automatically rule a property out. Share what you know on the form and we will review it.

Are there any fees or commissions?

No. There are no commissions or fees from us, and we pay the closing costs. The offer you accept is what you get.

How fast can you close on Florida land?

It depends mostly on the title work for your property. Many deals close in a few weeks, and you can pick a later date if that works better for you.
