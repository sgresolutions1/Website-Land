North Carolina land

We buy vacant land in North Carolina

If you own vacant land in North Carolina and want a straightforward cash sale, SGRE Solutions buys lots, wooded tracts and acreage across the state. We are a Miami-based land buyer that purchases vacant land nationwide, and we are glad to look at property in the Piedmont, on the coast, and in rural counties farther west or north.

In the Piedmont, we buy land in and around counties such as Davidson, Randolph, Rowan and Stanly, including communities like Lexington, Thomasville and Denton, in the corridor between Winston-Salem, Greensboro and Charlotte. Just northeast of Raleigh, counties such as Franklin, Vance, Granville and Warren have a mix of wooded land, former farmland and rural residential lots around towns like Louisburg, Youngsville and Henderson. On the coast, we look at vacant land in New Hanover, Brunswick, Onslow, Pender and Columbus counties, around Wilmington, Leland, Shallotte, Southport, Jacksonville and neighboring communities. Some of that land is in older subdivisions; some is wooded acreage; and some has wetlands or flood-zone questions that make a typical listing harder. We also consider rural mountain and foothill parcels in western North Carolina when the land is a fit.

Inherited, rural or hard-to-list NC land

A lot of North Carolina land has been in a family for years, or was inherited by someone who no longer lives nearby. Others are tired of holding costs, do not want to subdivide or list, or simply need a clean sale after a move, a divorce or a probate. You do not have to clear timber, improve a driveway, or get the land ready for showings in order to hear from us.

Share the county, acreage and anything you know about access or title, or call us and we will take it from there. We review nearby land sales and the details of your parcel, then make a cash offer. There are no commissions or fees from us, and we pay closing costs. We work through inherited property, probate, back taxes and title issues regularly. If you accept an offer, you choose the closing date that works for you.
