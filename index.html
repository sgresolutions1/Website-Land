FAQ

Common questions

Is there any obligation if I ask for an offer?

None at all. Requesting an offer is free. You see the number first, ask questions, and decide on your own time. If it isn’t a fit, just say no.

How is this different from listing with an agent?

An agent lists your land and takes a commission if it sells. We are the buyer. We purchase vacant land directly with cash and as-is — no commission, no showings, no waiting on a buyer’s loan.

Are there fees or closing costs?

You don’t pay us an agent commission or listing fee. Closing costs depend on the deal and local custom — we’ll be clear up front before you commit.

How do you come up with my offer?

We look at recent comparable land sales nearby, the size and access of your parcel, and what it would take for a buyer to use it. Cash offers are usually below a perfect retail sale — in exchange you skip time, fees, and uncertainty.

Do I need to clear or improve the land first?

No. We buy vacant land as-is in many conditions. Keep what matters to you and leave the rest.

What kinds of land do you buy?

Primarily vacant lots and acreage. Rural parcels, infill lots, and land owners who want a simple cash sale are a good fit. If you’re unsure, send the details — we’ll tell you honestly.
