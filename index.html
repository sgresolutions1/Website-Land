About us

About SGRE Solutions

SGRE Solutions LLC started in Miami, Florida in 2021 out of a simple love for real estate. To us, land is a blank canvas: whatever someone can imagine, a piece of land can make real. We started this company to help landowners turn property they no longer want into cash, so that land can go on to become something new.

Our mission is to make selling land simple, fair, and stress-free. We pay cash and cover all closing costs and fees, so what we offer is what you get. We buy vacant land anywhere in the United States, from small lots to large acreage, and we work through complicated situations. Title issues, probate, inherited property, and back taxes are all things we handle every day.

Based in Miami, FL. Buying land nationwide.

FAQ

Common questions

How do you come up with my offer?

We look at recent sales of similar land nearby, plus the size, access and location of your parcel, and make you a fair cash offer based on that.

Are there any fees or commissions?

No. There are no commissions or fees, and we pay all closing costs. The offer you accept is what you get.

How fast can you close?

It depends mostly on the title work for your property. Many deals close in a few weeks, and you can pick a later date if that works better for you.

What if there are back taxes, liens, title issues or probate?

We handle them. Back taxes, liens, title problems, inherited land and probate are things we work through every day.

Do I need to clean up the land or get a survey?

No. We buy land as-is. No cleanup, no survey and no improvements needed.

Where do you buy land?

Anywhere in the United States. We’re based in Miami, FL and buy land nationwide, including in Arizona, Florida, North Carolina and Georgia.

Is there any obligation if I ask for an offer?

None. Asking for an offer is free. If the offer isn’t a fit, just say no.
